Map tiles for Verteron (World_L_A), Altgard (World_D_A), Eltnen (World_L_B),
Morheim (World_D_B) and the three Reshanta levels (Abyss_Reshanta_A, C and D).

Source: AION2 Interactive Map by Yihao Liu (tc-imba)
https://github.com/aion2-interactive-map/aion2-interactive-map
Verteron and Altgard: public/UI/Map/WorldMap/World_L_A/Res and World_D_A/Res,
retrieved 2026-11-02. Eltnen, Morheim and Reshanta: the same pictures as served
by https://mmorpg-aion2.ru/interactive-map/ , retrieved 2026-11-17.

Each picture uses the same square as the interactive map. Verteron, Altgard,
Eltnen and Morheim are 8192 px from -408000 to 408000 cm. Lower Reshanta is
4096 px from -204000 to 204000 cm. Middle and Upper Reshanta are 2048 px from
-102000 to 102000 cm. Pixel = offset + scale * world centimeters, x to the
right and y downward, with no flip. scale = 8192/816000. Offsets are 4096,
2048 and 1024.

spawns.json lists every possible Hidden Cube spot (Verteron 1440, Altgard 1371,
Eltnen 912, Morheim 910) in packet centimeters. The Reshanta maps have no
hidden-cube spots. Source: https://mmorpg-aion2.ru/interactive-map/
A spot is drawn even after the cube has been collected.

kibelisks.json lists the "Телепорты и кибелиски" points from the same map,
also in packet centimeters. Map alignment clicks only these points. Their icons
are the map's own marker images:
maps/icons/kibelisk-elyos.webp, kibelisk-asmodian.webp and abyss-portal.webp.

flight-ring.webp is the "Полётные кольца" icon from the same map. flight-rings.json
lists those rings on Lower Reshanta (49) and Upper Reshanta (32). Middle Reshanta
has none. They are not used for map alignment.

hidden-cube.webp is the Hidden Cube icon from the same interactive map
(public/images/HiddenCube.webp), CC BY-NC 4.0.
