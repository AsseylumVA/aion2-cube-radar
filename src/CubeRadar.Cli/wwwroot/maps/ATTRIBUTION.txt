These 8192 px images are half the native world-map texture. Pixel = offset +
scale * world centimeters, x to the right and y downward, with no flip. The
Verteron numbers are half of the native fit for Steam build 25800038
(scale 0.01958351 px/cm, offset 8070.94, 8307.45 on the 16384 px map).

spawns.json lists every possible Hidden Cube spot (Verteron 1440, Altgard 1371)
in packet centimeters. Source: https://mmorpg-aion2.ru/interactive-map/
A spot is drawn even after the cube has been collected.

hidden-cube.webp is the Hidden Cube icon from the same interactive map
(public/images/HiddenCube.webp), CC BY-NC 4.0.
